### 2026-11-05 thu-shift
What coeliac-friendly venues get right: one person on every shift who knows the answer.

When a coeliac guest asks a question, the worst reply is a shrug and "I'll ask someone". The best is a calm answer from someone who already knows.

The venues that do this well tend to share three habits.

They keep the information to hand. Ingredient details and supplier information live in one place, and the whole team knows where.

They brief every shift. Not just the head chef. Whoever is on the floor tonight can answer, or knows exactly who can.

They keep the language simple. "Here's how we prepare it" beats "we do gluten free" every time.

None of it costs anything. It just needs doing before the first question, not after.

If your team already works like this, let coeliacs know. They tell each other.
