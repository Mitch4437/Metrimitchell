### 2026-11-12 honest

What coeliac-friendly venues get right: they say what they can't do.

The venues coeliacs recommend most are not always the ones who can do everything. They are the ones who are honest about it.

"We can do the burger on a gluten free bun, but the chips share a fryer." That one sentence is worth more than a menu full of symbols.

It lets the guest decide. It shows the kitchen has thought it through. And it means nobody gets an uncomfortable surprise at the table.

Three habits I see in the best answers:

They say what they can do, and what they can't.
They say it before the guest has to ask twice.
They say it the same way every shift.

Honesty doesn't cost anything. It builds the kind of reputation that coeliacs pass on to each other.

If your team already works like this, let coeliacs know.
