Poster: MYTH VS FACT / Gluten free dish doesn’t mean gluten free kitchen / The dish is only half the story. Ask how it’s made, not just what’s in it. / JOIN FREE

Myth: “It’s gluten free, so it’s fine.”

Fact: a gluten free dish and a gluten free kitchen are not the same thing. The ingredients might be fine. The question is what happens next: shared fryer, shared board, same tongs, same toaster.

So ask two things, not one:

What’s in it?
How is it made?

A place that answers both without blinking is one worth noting. Coeliac-Key is where we note them.

### 2026-10-29 thu-phone
What coeliac-friendly venues get right on the phone.

Before a coeliac books, they usually ring. That one call decides whether the table is yours. Here is what the best venues do.

They stay calm. “Of course, let me check with the kitchen” is the best answer there is. It tells the caller you take it seriously.

They get specific. What’s in the dish, how it’s cooked, what it’s cooked next to. Vague answers like “we do gluten free” don’t help anyone.

They offer to call back. A chef who checks properly beats a quick guess every time.

They never over-promise. “Here’s how we take care” earns far more confidence than a big claim.

None of this costs anything. It just needs the team to know the answer before the phone rings.
