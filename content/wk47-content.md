### 2026-11-19 thu-onepage

What coeliac-friendly venues get right: they write it down.

The kitchens coeliacs recommend most often don't leave it to one person's memory. They keep one page, near the pass, that says what is cooked where, which sauces and stocks contain flour, and who to ask.

It sounds dull. It works.

When a guest says "I'm coeliac", the answer doesn't depend on who's on shift or how busy the night is. Anyone can read the page and give the same calm, honest answer.

Three things I'd put on it:

What can be made without gluten, and how.
What can't, and why.
Who checks the packets when something is unclear.

It takes an hour to write and saves a hundred awkward conversations. Guests remember the places that sound sure of themselves, and coeliacs pass those names to each other.

If your team already works like this, let coeliacs know.
